Setting things up

Choose Allow in your browser's permission prompt. If it is hidden, check the microphone or site settings beside the address bar.

The audio engine and encrypted connection are starting. You can cancel with the large button.

Keep both devices online and the speaker page open. You can cancel and pair again if the connection does not finish.

Sound care

Speaker volume

70%

Start low, then increase gently. Your device's volume also affects the sound.

Keep some distance. A microphone near its speaker can cause echo or a loud squeal. Move them apart, turn the speaker down, or use headphones. Processing cannot guarantee feedback prevention.

Help

Two devices, one microphone

Connect speaker first

On the playback device, choose Speaker and press Start speaker. Scan its QR code with your microphone device. Or copy the link, paste it into Webmic on that device, and press Pair. Then press Start microphone and allow access.

Stay in control

Mute pauses microphone audio without ending the connection. Stop ends the session; the large button also cancels setup. Switching roles stops the current session. Opening this help does not interrupt audio.

Echo, squealing, or delay?

Keep the microphone away from the speaker, turn playback down, or use headphones. Browser audio processing is device-dependent and does not prevent speaker feedback. Bluetooth and network routing can add delay; use wired audio when possible.

Private pairing

Audio travels over encrypted iroh connections through relay servers. The QR code and pairing link grant access to this speaker; share them only with your microphone device. Stop and restart the speaker to replace the link.
