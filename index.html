Avoid feedback. A microphone near its speaker can cause echo or a loud squeal. Move them apart, lower the speaker volume, or use headphones. Audio processing cannot guarantee prevention.

Help

Connect speaker first

Choose Speaker on the playback device and press Start speaker. Scan its QR code with your microphone device, or paste its link into Webmic and press Pair. Then press Start microphone and allow access.

Session controls

Mute pauses audio without disconnecting. Stop ends the session; Cancel setup stops a pending connection. Switching roles stops the session. Help does not interrupt audio.

Echo, squealing, or delay?

Move the microphone away from the speaker, lower playback volume, or use headphones. Browser processing cannot prevent speaker feedback. Bluetooth and network routing can add delay; use wired audio when possible.

Private pairing

Audio uses encrypted iroh connections through relay servers. Anyone with the QR code or pairing link can connect to this speaker. Share it only with your microphone device. Stop and restart the speaker to replace the link.
